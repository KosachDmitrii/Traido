# ORB investigation — 30 September 2026

## Evidence

Production saved sessions and Alpaca SIP M5 bars were replayed with the active
completed-bar policy. Each evaluation uses only its completed prefix. No live
quote, broker fill, price-path P&L, or profitability was simulated. Missing
durable intervals remain blocked; selected plans only are available for replay.
Historical bars can contain subsequent provider corrections, so replay is not
an exact reconstruction of the provider response originally seen by the process.

BUY_ALLOWED is an ORB quote-trigger evaluation, not final admission, a distinct
opportunity, an order, or a fill. Repeated evaluations must not be counted as
distinct trades. Three trigger evaluations have no corresponding BLOCKED count;
these aggregates do not establish their eventual outcome.

50 selected symbol/session histories contain missing M5 intervals; absence can
mean no trades or incomplete recording. These intervals were not filled with
synthetic candles. The 164 confirmations are a conservative replay result for
the recorded universe, not all conceivable market opportunities.

## Recorded refusal attribution

Of 359 BLOCKED events, 342 contain REGIME_TIMESTAMP_MISSING (five explicitly
also contain FRED_OBSERVATION_STALE), 16 contain
SECTOR_METADATA_MISSING,SECTOR_ASSESSMENT_MISSING, and one contains only ADP.
Older generic timestamp errors do not establish which individual FRED series
was unavailable or stale. Current diagnosis requires a fresh source probe.

The production context probe at 2026-09-30 15:13 UTC returned healthy, tradable
macro facts: DGS10 5.24 dated 2026-09-28 and UNRATE 4.10 dated 2026-08-01.
Monthly observation freshness already uses calendar months in current HEAD;
the historical missing-timestamp block was not reproduced by this fresh probe.

Therefore the previous explanation that no conditions arose for two weeks was
incomplete: confirmed setups arose, and mandatory downstream context checks
blocked recorded attempts. Lowering ATR, volume or R/R would not repair a missing
macro timestamp or a missing instrument classification.

## Confirmed code defect and repair

`retest.rebuild` regenerated the base plan with `form_plan` and retained only
reentry metadata. This discarded selection instrument classification and policy
migration provenance. Broad ETF classifications can consequently fail at the
sector resolver because company-profile data is not a reliable fund classifier.

The replay now preserves independent provenance while rebuilding derived retest
geometry. Discovery restores already-missing classification on unclaimed plans
from the real eligible provider universe under the same session lock used by
publication. Published, approving, executed and unresolved claims are unchanged.
No entry geometry, risk gate, quote requirement or trading threshold is relaxed.

Blocked final-admission cards are retained in the potential-entry view with an
explicit blocked status and translated reasons, including macro and sector
failures. Display membership does not authorize a purchase.

## Read-only diagnostic surfaces

- `/api/v1/diagnostics/orb-replay?session=YYYY-MM-DD`: saved-bar replay and recorded
  observation aggregates.
- Add `replay_bars=false` to inspect recorded reason aggregates without replay.
- `/api/v1/diagnostics/orb-context`: current FRED assessment and macro gate,
  without broker access or order creation.

PostgreSQL's deployed JSON columns are grouped through an explicit text cast;
grouping JSON directly is not supported by PostgreSQL.

## Verification and limits

The existing ORB suite passed 126 tests before changes. New checks cover completed
prefixes, future-bar exclusion, missing history, setup deduplication, read-only
reporting, grouped reasons, retained provenance, repair idempotency and preservation
of UNKNOWN claims. Targeted suites passed after the repairs. Python lint/format
checks passed. This audit does not certify profitability or validate a proposed
alternative strategy. A stale mandatory macro source must be repaired at the
source rather than assigned a fabricated timestamp.
